Team photos.

The five photos here have been processed to match: cropped square to head and
shoulders, converted to a navy duotone, and saved at 400x400.

The originals came from five completely different settings — white studio, blue
background, outdoors, dark curtain, black and white. Left untouched they looked
like five unrelated pictures stuck in a row. The duotone makes them read as one
team.

ADDING SOMEONE NEW
Send the photo through the same treatment, or the row stops matching:
  1. Crop square, head and shoulders, face filling most of the frame
  2. Convert to greyscale
  3. Tint: shadows to #0C203E, highlights to #F6F9FD
  4. Save 400x400 JPEG

Then add them to the `team` array in src/data/site.js with
  image: './assets/team/name.jpg'

Leave `image` empty and initials show instead — fine temporarily, but a row
with some faces and some initials looks unfinished. Use all photos or none.
